Submission system

The map nodes above sit on this stable execution path; audits and controls test stages rather than changing every stage.

1. DiscoverFind train, test, and sample submission; infer ID and target.
2. Quick valid CSVWrite a schema-preserving fallback before expensive work.
3. PortfolioGenerate bounded model and rank-blend candidates.
4. Optional adviserOnly target-blind, allowlisted changes with a direct gate.
5. Harness selectionSubmit and select candidates before time expires.

Stage changes: v1-v5 established discovery/submission reliability; v6/v9 changed the portfolio selector; v7/v8/v10/v11 tested specialists and features; v12 established the five-stage portfolio; v13 changed stage 4 safely; v15 capped stage 3 runtime; v14 was an attributed governance control.

Decision timeline

  1. 9 Jul

    What: Reviewed official/public examples and tried three custom shells.

    Why: Establish a valid agent ZIP and mandatory submission behavior.

    Result: All custom variants failed at runtime; the extracted official demo became the 0.815 baseline.

    Public base: Official demo; A-to-Z and dynamic-AutoML guides.

  2. 25–28 Jul

    What: Built a reliable blend then pick-best selector; ran CatBoost, AutoGluon, Mitra and feature-family replays.

    Why: Improve tabular quality without losing the first valid candidate.

    Result: v6/v9 both reached 0.819. Full 16-task evidence rejected standalone CatBoost, AutoGluon and broad features as defaults.

    Public base: Design patterns only; no public score is attributed to these submissions.

  3. 29–30 Jul

    What: Wired an LLM plan (v10), then added a gated native-CatBoost candidate (v11).

    Why: Test targeted feature choice and categorical specialization.

    Result: Live 0.808 and 0.810. The v10 gate controlled a sub-decision, not final candidate selection; synthetic CatBoost gains did not transfer.

  4. 31 Jul

    What: Adopted a quick fallback plus deterministic multi-candidate portfolio and bounded Pro freeroll (v12).

    Why: Full solution-blind replay favored diversity and validated output/runtime guards.

    Result: 0.80288 replay full AUC under simulated public selection and 0.822 live — the recorded best.

    Public base: Adapted Naji and Kun Zhang portfolio designs with attribution.

  5. 1–4 Aug

    What: Tested target-blind planning (v13), seed/runtime insurance (v15), and an exact public freeroll control (v14).

    Why: Determine whether controlled LLM use, variance reduction, or freeroll governance had remaining headroom.

    Result: All completed at 0.822. Six further levers were null/negative; the recorded Sonnet retry remained pending.

    Public base: v14 was a static, attributed extraction; it was not executed locally.
