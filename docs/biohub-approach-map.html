Lesson

Both August plateaus broke on adopted external components, and the failure modes are the lesson. A config diff is not a port: lifting three keys without the filters that made them survivable produced cells dividing into three. An old negative can be an artifact: a division veto that rejected every candidate was loading the wrong checkpoint. And a public score is not transferable evidence: forking the 0.923 recipe verbatim scored 0.918, exactly what porting two of its filters had already achieved.
