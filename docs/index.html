Biohub — Cell Tracking During Development
Kaggle · Map · Timeline		Detect cells in 3D microscopy, link them across time, and identify divisions. Scored as adjusted edge Jaccard + 0.1 × division Jaccard.DoG/Hungarian baseline; U-Net + transformer + ILP; association MLP ranker, harmonic fusion, TTA; density-adaptive relink gates and a DeepCenter mitosis veto. Exact KD-tree rewrite of the relink assignment (byte-identical output, 25× faster). Post-hoc division repair explored to exhaustion and closed on evidence.Below some effect size, stop ranking candidates and start diversifying. Private scores inverted the offline ranking: of 15 submissions tied at public 0.947, private spread 0.913–0.916, and the two configurations the harness ranked highest scored lowest. The baseline they were measured against beat them, and the two finally selected were chosen as first-and-second by that ranking. Public and private correlated at r = 0.863 across 49 submissions — strong in the large, useless at the 0.001 resolution being optimised at, because the held-out gains were real on held-out training videos and did not survive the change of distribution. Two picks chosen for dissimilarity would have beaten two chosen by rank. Earlier in the same project the reverse lesson held and still does: aggregate deltas over-credit a change that moves few samples, and restricting paired tests to affected samples correctly rejected a candidate the aggregate endorsed. Both are the same underlying point — know your instrument's resolution, and stop optimising below it. Also: three separate quantitative predictions here (a division ceiling, a runtime projection, a predicted leaderboard drop) were directionally right and numerically off by 3×, 2× and 4×; treat such estimates as reasons a direction is closed, never as forecasts of how far anything moves.Finished; 29 Sep 2026
