Biohub competition approach timeline

Chronological decision record through 14 August 2026. The flow map shows dependencies; this view shows when evidence arrived and which paths were closed.

Biohub — Cell Tracking During Development. This competition asks participants to reconstruct a family tree of cells from a sequence of 3D microscope images. It is ongoing; entered 3 July 2026; scheduled finish 29 September 2026. Kaggle overview.

What is submitted?

For every movie frame, we submit cell-centre coordinates (nodes) and links from each cell to its likely descendant in the next frame (edges). A cell with two descendants is a predicted division.

What does the score mean?

Higher is better. The main term rewards correct lineage links (adjusted edge Jaccard); a smaller term rewards correct division events. A public score is Kaggle’s result on a hidden subset, while local validation uses labelled training movies.

What is the pipeline?

Find cells in each 3D image → propose links between consecutive frames → select a biologically consistent graph → make conservative repairs. Most experiments change one of these stages rather than replacing the whole system.

How to read the arrows

Each arrow names the experiment that supplied the next baseline or motivated a follow-up test. Red cards are paths closed by evidence; dashed amber cards were intentionally held back rather than submitted.

3 July — establish a working tracker

Exact DoG + Hungarian

What
Detected bright cell-like spots with classical image processing, then linked each spot to one successor with Hungarian assignment.
Why
Establish a simple end-to-end tracker and a valid submission baseline.
Result
Public 0.827.

NMS 3.8 µm

What
Increased non-maximum suppression, which merges nearby duplicate cell detections, to 3.8 µm.
Why
Test whether reducing duplicate detections improves linking without changing the tracker.
Result
Local 0.8105; public 0.834. Adopted.

U-Net + transformer + ILP

What
Used a learned cell detector and learned link scorer, then an ILP global optimizer to select a consistent lineage graph.
Why
Test a full learned replacement for the classical tracker.
Result
Public 0.810, below the simpler tracker; closed.

Gap-2 bridges

What
Proposed links across a two-frame absence, inserting a missing intermediate cell position.
Why
Recover lineages when a cell is missed briefly by the detector.
Result
Local validation regressed; deliberately not submitted.

What

16–20 July — calibrate and probe the graph

Exp073 calibration

What
Retuned detection confidence, removed implausibly short tracks, disabled longer gap repairs, and retained conservative divisions.
Why
Calibrate the adopted tracker one setting at a time for cleaner lineages.
Result
Public 0.903; became the working recipe.

Exp084–108 calibration sweeps

What
Swept detection threshold, density-aware gap rules, short-track handling, gap placement, and division gates.
Why
Look for a local improvement around the Exp073 recipe.
Result
Public 0.902–0.903; plateau, so the knobs were closed.

Exp110 ILP costs

What
Changed the global optimizer's penalty for starting or ending a track, yielding a smaller graph.
Why
Test whether less permissive lineage formation removes incorrect links.
Result
Public 0.909; new working best.

Exp112–116 ILP variants

What
Swept ILP disappearance penalties and tested exporting the raw optimizer graph without the usual repairs.
Why
Test whether graph density or the post-processing layer caused the Exp110 gain.
Result
Penalty sweep 0.908–0.909; direct export 0.877. Closed.

27 July–2 August — fusion becomes the backbone

Exp148 adaptive fusion

What
Blended detections from two independently trained models and let the more reliable link prediction dominate for ambiguous links.
Why
Add diversity without running two full trackers or changing every stage at once.
Result
Public 0.913; improved backbone.

Trackastra replacement

What
Replaced the incumbent linking algorithm with a specialised tracking model.
Why
Test whether a different association model could correct hard links.
Result
Failed locally and scored public 0.898; closed.

Division quantity and ranking

What
Changed how many division events were admitted and ranked them with image or transformer signals.
Why
Improve the smaller but valuable division component without disrupting ordinary links.
Result
Public 0.905–0.913; no improvement over Exp148.

Association repairs

What
Tried targeted edge swaps, future-aware re-linking, and harmonic-score-only alternatives.
Why
Repair ambiguous parent-child assignments after the main graph was built.
Result
Tied at 0.913 or yielded no viable changes; closed.
