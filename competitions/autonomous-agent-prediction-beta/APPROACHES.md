### 2026-07-30 — v11 pick-best plus gated CatBoost (submission `55105170`)

| Date | Approach | Changes | Local validation | Public LB | Outcome | Follow-up |
| --- | --- | --- | --- | --- | --- | --- |
| 2026-07-30 | `official-demo-v11-pick-best-plus-catboost` | Rebuilt from `official-demo-v9-pick-best-model` (0.819). Adds CatBoost as a **gated 5th candidate** in the pick-best-OOF selector. Two design details established by measurement, not assumption: (1) CatBoost gets a **native-categorical view** (`cat_features` + raw categorical columns) rather than the target/frequency encodings the sklearn trees use; (2) it must beat the best sklearn model by **>= 0.003 OOF** to be promoted, while the four sklearn models keep v9's bare argmax. Guarded import, `thread_count=1`, `iterations=400`, `allow_writing_files=False`, and a 60s minimum-time guard so it never starts and gets cut off mid-folds. | 4 synthetic regimes: large-numeric 7200 rows (cat rejected, test 0.96477 = v9), small 900 rows (rejected, 0.95861 = v9), numeric-only edge (rejected, no crash with `cat_features=[]`), **high-cardinality categorical 5400 rows (cat promoted, test 0.83271 vs 0.82758 = +0.0051)**. Extracted ZIP diffed identical to source and re-run to reproduce the gain. | **0.810** | Submission `55105170` completed, regressing 0.009 from the v6/v9 live best. | **Rejected as the live default.** Unlike v10, the gate controlled the model that wrote the sole submission, but its synthetic categorical gain still did not generalize to the hidden evaluation. Require broader replay evidence before revisiting CatBoost promotion. |

**Two measurements worth keeping from building this:**

1. **Target-encoding categoricals before CatBoost wastes it.** On the categorical task, CatBoost scored 0.80087 OOF on the encoded view (losing to LR's 0.80625) and 0.81568 on the native view (winning by +0.0094). Handing CatBoost the same encoded matrix as the sklearn trees makes it "just another GBDT" — which is exactly the configuration that measured a tie with AutoGluon in the 2026-07-27 work. The earlier "CatBoost ties everything else" conclusion may have been partly an artifact of the encoded feature view.
2. **A bare argmax over K models is exposed to winner's curse.** On the 900-row task CatBoost won OOF by 0.00044 and *lost* 0.0028 on held-out test. Measured OOF noise here is ~0.0004, so an argmax can promote a fragile model on noise alone. Genuine CatBoost wins were ~0.01, two orders larger, so a 0.003 margin separates signal from noise cleanly.
